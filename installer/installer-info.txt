Cryptmaster Whisper

Adds Whisper as an optional voice recogniser for Cryptmaster's voice input. Windows Speech Recognition,
the game's own (default; vanilla), stays the default: switch to Whisper in BepInEx\config\
cryptmaster.whisper.cfg (Engine = Whisper), and back to Windows if Whisper misbehaves.

Whisper mode is an attempt to use a feature the original game shipped but never used. It makes no
promise of better recognition than Windows Speech Recognition.

What this installs:
- the mod, into BepInEx\plugins\CryptmasterWhisper;
- BepInEx 5 (the mod loader), if it isn't installed yet;
- whisper.cpp built with Vulkan (works on AMD, Intel and NVIDIA GPUs, and on the CPU without one);
- optionally, a larger Whisper model, downloaded from its official source;
- optionally, on PCs with an NVIDIA GPU, a CUDA backend, downloaded from its official source;
- the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime, downloaded from Microsoft, only if it's missing.

Downloads are checked against fixed SHA-256 hashes. Nothing is sent anywhere: recognition runs entirely
on your computer.

Uninstalling removes the mod's folder. BepInEx and the mod's settings stay, since other mods may use them.
